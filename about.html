About CivicConnect

CivicConnect is a smart web-based platform designed to help citizens report civic issues quickly and efficiently. It connects the public with government departments to improve transparency and faster issue resolution.

Our Mission

To empower citizens by providing an easy-to-use platform for reporting civic problems and improving communication with local government departments.

Our Vision

To build cleaner, safer and smarter cities through digital participation, transparency and efficient complaint management.

Why Choose CivicConnect?

Location Based Reporting

Automatically capture complaint location for faster response.

Photo Evidence

Upload images while reporting issues for better verification.

Real-Time Updates

Receive instant notifications about complaint progress.

Community Participation

Support and comment on nearby complaints to improve civic engagement.

Issue Reporting and Resolution System

This project is developed as an MCA Final Year Project. It enables citizens to report issues like garbage, potholes, water leakage and streetlight failures. The system routes the complaint to the respective department where officials can update the complaint status and upload proof after resolution.
