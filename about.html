About CivicConnect Platform

Empowering citizens and municipal departments through geotagged grievance reporting, automated department routing, and verified resolution tracking.

Our Mission

To provide a transparent, accountable, and accessible digital portal for citizens to report civic grievances. CivicConnect automates department assignment and verifies issue resolution using geotagged EXIF photo metadata.

Our Vision

Building cleaner, safer, and responsive urban environments through modern technology, real-time status notifications, OpenStreetMap GIS tracking, and strict municipal service-level agreements.

Core Technology Highlights

EXIF GPS Parsing

Extracts latitude and longitude directly from uploaded camera photos.

OpenStreetMap GIS

Interactive Leaflet maps rendering complaint & resolution markers.

Real-time Push

Instant toast alerts on status updates and department remarks.

JWT Security

Role-based access control protecting Citizen, Department, and Admin panels.
