Municipal Grievance Categories & SLAs

CivicConnect guarantees automated department routing and time-bound resolution tracking

Road Department

Handles pothole repairs, road damage, paving, and sidewalk maintenance.

Target SLA: 48 Hours
Sanitation Department

Handles uncollected garbage, open waste dumps, public bin clearing.

Target SLA: 24 Hours
Electrical Department

Handles dark stretches, broken street lamp fixtures, hazardous wiring.

Target SLA: 24 Hours
Water Department

Handles pipeline bursts, main leakages, sewage blockages.

Target SLA: 36 Hours
